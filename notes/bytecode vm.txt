Bytecode VM: Blinx 2 runs on a custom scripting engine (SRCADV/SRCED/SRCAC). Game logic is likely scripted, not compiled C++. This is actually good for AI decompilation — scripting VMs are simpler to analyze and the scripts themselves (stored in the .ipk archives) may be extractable.
